# 2. PRODUCT BACKGROUND

Alumates Pro is a digital ecosystem built to connect people and businesses across the interior design, architecture, construction, and technology industries.

The industry is fragmented. People looking to learn, hire professionals, find jobs, work on projects, or source materials often depend on separate platforms and communication channels.

Alumates Pro brings these activities together in one platform.

**Product positioning:**

"Learn. Hire. Build. Work — All in One."

**Core product proposition:**
