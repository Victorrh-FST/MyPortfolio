## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, confident, editorial digital aesthetic tailored for high-impact product design showcases, modern creative agencies, and cutting-edge portfolio platforms. It balances stark Swiss-inspired architectural discipline with digital dynamism, evoking clarity, elite precision, and deliberate poise.

The visual style blends **Minimalism** with subtle **Neo-Digital Precision**:
- High-contrast, punchy headline hierarchies paired with whisper-quiet, off-white surface layers.
- Expansive negative space that frames content like gallery installations.
- Strategic chromatic bursts via an electric cobalt blue highlight that commands instant focus to hyperlinks, active states, and calls to action.
- Soft, generous structural geometry (oversized card radiuses, crisp pill badges) that grounds the technical tone with inviting physical softness.

## Colors

The palette relies on deliberate contrast between a warm-tinted gallery canvas, pure ink-black typography, and a hyper-saturated cobalt blue:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:** The primary ambient canvas is `#F3F3F2`, avoiding harsh stark whites in favor of an editorial, warm-tinted foundation. Elevated modules, showcase tiles, and testimonial cards employ `#EBEBEA` or pure `#FFFFFF` with hairline borders for soft definition.
- **Ink & Typography:** Primary text and dark hero containers utilize `#060606` for maximum typographic impact. Secondary copy and metadata labels rely on `#71717A` (and `#929294` on dark footers) to establish clear hierarchy without visual clutter.
- **Electric Accent:** The primary chromatic anchor is `#201DE2`, an electric cobalt that sparks interactive engagement. It serves exclusively for key textual emphases (e.g., inline hero company links), primary pill buttons, focus rings, and active navigation indicators.
- **Pill Badges:** Micro-categorization chips use `#18181B` with pure white `#FFFFFF` text to act as architectural punctuation marks beneath project mockups.

## Typography

The design system exclusively leverages **Manrope**, harnessing its geometric open counters and balanced modern proportions to unify display headlines, body storytelling, and micro-labels under a single cohesive voice.

- **Hero & Section Openers:** Hero statements use generous negative letter tracking (`-0.03em`) and medium weights (`500` to `600`) to evoke a clean editorial presence rather than an aggressive heavy-weight stance.
- **Accent Highlighting:** Inline headline keywords (e.g. past company tenures, pivotal outcomes) shift color directly into `#201DE2` without changing font weight, delivering an effortless narrative focus.
- **Legibility & Rhythm:** Subheadings and descriptors maintain high readability through muted zinc tones (`#71717A`), preserving a strict separation between dominant ideas and explanatory context.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a **Fluid Structural Grid** with high outer canvas margins, breathing room, and strict proportional alignment:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width containment at `1280px` with 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `3rem` (`48px`) margins. Section gaps range from `5rem` to `7rem` (`80px`–`112px`) to emphasize showcase pacing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer padding. Project and testimonial cards reflow into equal 2-column or stacked 1-column layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer margins. Hero font scales to `headline-hero-mobile`, and project preview grids collapse into a focused, vertical single-column stream.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy layered dropshadows, leaning instead on **Tonal Layers**, **Ultra-Fine Hairlines**, and **Tactile Glassmorphism**:

- **Floating Navigation Bar:** Floating dock elevation is achieved via a subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), combined with an off-white translucent fill (`rgba(243, 243, 242, 0.85)`), a `1px` translucent border (`rgba(228, 228, 231, 0.6)`), and an ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Showcase Cards:** Project cards rely primarily on flat surface differentiation (`#EBEBEA` fill against `#F3F3F2` background). When elevated hover interactions occur, apply a subtle ambient bloom: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Testimonial Containers:** Flat, quiet containers using `#EBEBEA` with zero shadow and a crisp `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)` perimeter boundary to prevent visual noise.
- **Footer Slab:** An inverted full-bleed block (`#060606`) creates a grounded anchor at the base of the viewport.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an expressive, fluid shape system characterized by ultra-rounded container corners paired with full geometric capsules:

- **Showcase Cards & Large Panels:** Use expansive corners (`1.75rem` / `28px` to `2rem` / `32px`), giving large mockups and testimonial tiles an approachable, pebble-like contour.
- **Device Frames & Inner Previews:** Hero images and embedded device screenshots nested within cards scale gracefully with `1.25rem` (`20px`) to `1.5rem` (`24px`) radii.
- **Badges, Buttons & Navigation:** Styled as pure geometric pills (`border-radius: 9999px`), creating tactile interaction affordances across the entire UI.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill CTA):** Full capsule shape (`rounded-full`), background `#201DE2`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-nav`. Padding: `0.625rem 1.5rem`. Smooth hover brightness shift (`#1916BE`) with a micro-scale of `1.02`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Full capsule, background transparent, text `#060606`, padding: `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Subtle hover fill of `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

### Navigation Dock
- **Floating Island:** Centered floating container (`max-width: 900px`), height `56px`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), fixed or sticky top positioning with a `1.5rem` top offset. Houses the brand signature mark on the left, horizontal text links in the center (`#060606`, muted `#71717A` hover), and the primary contact pill button on the right.

### Badges & Micro-Pills
- **Meta Tags:** Capsule badges (`height: 24px`, padding: `0.25rem 0.75rem`, `border-radius: 9999px`). Background `#18181B`, text `#FFFFFF`, typography `label-badge`. Used in horizontal rows for project taxonomy (e.g., `2025`, `mobile app`).

### Showcase Cards
- **Project Showcase:** Large rounded container (`rounded-3xl` / `28px`), padding `1.5rem` to `2rem`. Contains an upper device framing canvas featuring a 3D-angled or neutral-padded mock, followed by a bottom metadata dock featuring taxonomy pills and a clear project title + outcome headline in `headline-md`.
- **Testimonial Card:** Rectangular card with `rounded-3xl` corners, soft background `#EBEBEA`, padding `2rem`. Displays quote copy in `quote-body` followed by author credentials in `body-sm` (`#71717A`).

### Form Elements & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Soft off-white surface (`#EBEBEA` or pure `#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E4E4E7`, `rounded-2xl`, height `48px`, padding `0 1.25rem`. Focus ring triggers a crisp `2px solid #201DE2` outline with zero blur spread.
