01. The Strategic Problem

The Fragmentation of West Africa’s Interior Design & Building Economy

In the emerging creative and interior design sector in Nigeria and West Africa, the journey of building a career or delivering a project was fractured across disjointed digital spaces:

“Learning, hiring, finding projects, and sourcing materials happen across separate platforms. This makes it difficult for students, professionals, and customers to navigate the industry efficiently.”

Students studied theory on foreign platforms with non-localized curricula; certified designers struggled to verify their credentials to prospective clients; homeowners turned to unvetted social media pages for artisans; and trade suppliers relied on haphazard peer recommendations.

The Disjointed Reality (Before)

Silos, Lost Revenue & Zero Verification

  • ✕ Generic Foreign LMS: High tuition, irrelevant building codes, and no local material context.
  • ✕ WhatsApp Group Chaos: Jobs shared across unsearchable chat threads without escrows or briefs.
  • ✕ Instagram DM Sourcing: Unverified fabricators and POP artisans with volatile pricing and ghosting risks.
High friction • Zero continuity from learning to earning
The Alumates Pro Solution

One Unified Vertical Platform

  • ✓ Localized Certified Academy: West African context, free preview module, automated certificate generation.
  • ✓ Tiered Verified Directory: Graduating students transition directly into listed Level-based practitioner profiles.
  • ✓ Marketplace & Business Directory: Direct bidding on construction briefs and verified vendor supply catalogs.
Full Lifecycle: Learn → Get Certified → Get Hired → Procure

Target Audience & Value Alignment

1. Students & Starters

03. Full-Stack Bubble Architecture

Relational Data Modeling Without Code

Building complex, high-traffic SaaS systems on Bubble.io requires rigorous database normalization, privacy rules, and state management. Below is the structural schema powering Alumates Pro.

Data Type: Course & LMS
  • • Course (title, price, Level)
  • • Module (course_id, order, is_free)
  • • Lesson (module_id, audio_file, pdf)
  • • QuizQuestion (options, correct_id)
  • • UserCorrectAnswers (user, score)
Data Type: Profiles & Trade
  • • TechProfile (skills, portfolio_imgs)
  • • TradeProfile (trade_type, badge)
  • • Supply Item (price, vendor, stock)
  • • ResumeFile (file_url, verified_flag)
  • • TrainingEvent (date, attendee_list)
Data Type: Subscription & User
  • • User (AppRole, completedLesson)
  • • Subscription (tier: Classic|Pro)
  • • Thread (messages, participants)
  • • Referral_Click (affiliate tracking)
  • • Referral_Signup (conversion count)

Paystack Webhook & Course Entitlement State Machine

01. Initialization

User clicks "Enroll (₦30,000)". Paystack popup initializes with Bubble user ID & course metadata.

02. Webhook Event

Paystack triggers charge.success to Bubble Backend API Workflow endpoint, ensuring fail-safe receipt.

03. DB Mutation

Bubble adds Course to Current User's EnrolledCourses. Modules 2–4 locks change from is_locked=yes to no.

04. Trigger Email

SendGrid fires receipt, onboarding syllabus guide, and auto-invitation to associate Slack/WhatsApp community.

05. Reflections & Engineering Decisions

What I Learned Designing for Emerging Creative Economies

1. Pragmatic Communication beats Platform Lock-in

Initially, we considered building an internal messaging chat room to keep all transactions on-platform. However, user interviews and observational research proved that tradespeople and clients in West Africa conduct business on WhatsApp. Instead of fighting user inertia, I integrated deep-link WhatsApp Web actions with pre-populated order briefs. Friction dropped immediately.

2. No-Code Requires True Software Engineering Mindset

Bubble makes visual UI quick to construct, but without normalized database architecture, page performance degrades rapidly with 1,780+ records. Building relational references between Courses, Lesson Progress, and Quiz Scores instead of deeply nested lists ensured sub-second loading states across the entire LMS.

3. The Courage to Deprecate Non-Core Features

Retiring the early AI design consultant was an invaluable lesson in product discipline. Just because an API is technologically feasible does not mean it solves the core user need. Removing it preserved focus on what truly generated value: certification and job matching.
